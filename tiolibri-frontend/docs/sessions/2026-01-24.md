## Zmiany w Supabase

### Tabele

| Tabela | Kolumny | RLS |
|--------|---------|-----|
| projects | id, user_id, title, author, language, status, style_preset, custom_styles, created_at, updated_at | user_id = auth.uid() |
| chapters | id, project_id, title, sort_order, source_file_path, processed_html, created_at, updated_at | przez project ownership |
| assets | id, project_id, name, type, file_path, created_at | przez project ownership |
| generated_files | id, project_id, format, file_path, file_size, generated_at | przez project ownership |

### RLS Policies (16 total)

- 4 na `projects` (SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE)
- 4 na `chapters`
- 4 na `assets`
- 4 na `generated_files`

### Storage Buckets

| Bucket | Limit | MIME Types | Status |
|--------|-------|------------|--------|
| uploads | 10 MB | text/html, application/xhtml+xml | Utworzony |
| assets | 5 MB | image/*, svg+xml | Utworzony |
| outputs | 50 MB | application/epub+zip, application/pdf | Utworzony |

### Storage Policies (12 total)

- 4 na każdy bucket (SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE)
- Sprawdzanie własności: `(storage.foldername(name))[1] IN (SELECT id::text FROM projects WHERE user_id = auth.uid())`

### Zmienne środowiskowe

```env
VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://[project-id].supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=[anon-key]
VITE_API_URL=http://localhost:8000
```
